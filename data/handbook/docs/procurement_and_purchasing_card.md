# Procurement and Purchasing Card Policy

*Brightwater Institute — Procurement Services Policy FIN-410. Fictional sample document written for the RAG demo.*

## Purchasing card limits

The purchasing card (P-card) is for small purchases of goods. The limit for a single transaction is 3,500 dollars and the monthly limit per card is 10,000 dollars. A purchase may not be split into several transactions to stay under the limit.

The following may not be bought with a P-card: gift cards, alcohol, personal items, cash advances, and fuel for personal vehicles. Computers and tablets are always bought through the IT standard catalog, whatever their price.

## Purchases above the card limit

Purchases between 3,500 and 25,000 dollars require a purchase order and two written quotes. Purchases between 25,000 and 100,000 dollars require three written quotes. Purchases above 100,000 dollars require a formal competitive bid managed by Procurement Services. A sole-source justification form is required for any purchase above 25,000 dollars that is made without competing quotes.

## Reconciliation

Cardholders reconcile every transaction in SpendTrack by the 10th day of the following month and attach a receipt for each one. The Institute is exempt from state sales tax, and cardholders present the exemption certificate at the time of purchase.

## Misuse

A first violation of this policy results in mandatory retraining. A second violation results in suspension of the card for six months. Fraudulent use of a card results in termination of employment and referral to campus police.
