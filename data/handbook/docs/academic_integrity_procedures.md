# Academic Integrity Procedures

*Brightwater Institute — Office of Academic Integrity. Fictional sample document written for the RAG demo.*

## What counts as a violation

Violations include plagiarism, cheating on examinations, fabrication of data or sources, unauthorized collaboration, and the use of generative AI tools where the course syllabus prohibits it.

## Reporting and response

An instructor who suspects a violation reports it to the Office of Academic Integrity within 10 business days of discovering it. The Office notifies the student in writing within 5 business days of receiving the report. The student may submit a written response within 7 calendar days of the notice. A student may not drop the course while a case is pending.

Contested cases are heard by a panel of two faculty members and one student. The panel decides by majority vote using a preponderance-of-the-evidence standard.

## Sanctions

For a first offense, sanctions range from a grade reduction on the assignment to a failing grade in the course, together with a required academic integrity seminar. A second offense results in suspension for at least one semester. A failing grade assigned for an integrity violation is recorded on the transcript as XF. The X notation is removed one year after the student completes the integrity seminar.

## Appeals

A student may appeal a decision to the Academic Integrity Appeals Board within 10 business days of the decision. The grounds for appeal are procedural error, new evidence, or a sanction that is disproportionate to the violation. The Board issues its decision within 30 days, and that decision is final.

## Records

Records of integrity cases are kept for seven years after the student graduates or leaves the Institute.
