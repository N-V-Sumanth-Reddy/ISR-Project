# Paid Leave and Holidays Policy

*Brightwater Institute — Human Resources Policy HR-204. Fictional sample document written for the RAG demo.*

## Vacation leave

Full-time staff accrue vacation leave every month. Employees in their first five years of service accrue 10 hours per month. Employees with more than five years of service accrue 12 hours per month, and employees with more than ten years of service accrue 14 hours per month. Part-time employees who work at least 20 hours per week accrue leave in proportion to their appointment.

Unused vacation leave carries over from year to year up to a maximum of 240 hours. Any balance above 240 hours is forfeited on September 1 of each year. Vacation requests longer than three working days must be entered in PeopleHub at least two weeks before the first day of leave. Supervisors answer requests within five working days.

## Sick leave

Full-time staff accrue 8 hours of sick leave per month. There is no cap on the sick leave balance. Sick leave may be used for the employee's own illness or to care for an immediate family member. A note from a health care provider is required for any absence longer than three consecutive working days. Unused sick leave is not paid out when an employee leaves the Institute.

## Parental leave

Staff who are the primary caregiver of a newborn or newly adopted child receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement. Employees become eligible after six months of continuous service.

## Bereavement and jury duty

Employees receive up to five paid days of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member and one paid day for other relatives. Employees called for jury duty are paid their regular salary for the full length of their service and must give their supervisor a copy of the summons.

## Holidays

The Institute observes 11 paid holidays each year. In addition, the Institute closes for a winter break from December 24 through January 1. Staff who are required to work on a paid holiday receive holiday pay plus an equal amount of compensatory time.
