# Graduate Assistantship Handbook

*Brightwater Institute — Graduate School. Fictional sample document written for the RAG demo.*

## Appointments and hours

A standard graduate assistantship is a 50 percent appointment, which means 20 hours of work per week. Graduate assistants may not work more than 20 hours per week in total across all Institute jobs while classes are in session. During official breaks they may work up to 40 hours per week.

## Stipends and tuition

The minimum stipend is 2,400 dollars per month for doctoral students and 2,100 dollars per month for master's students. The tuition waiver covers up to 9 credit hours in the fall and spring semesters and up to 6 credit hours in the summer. Mandatory fees are not covered by the waiver. The Institute pays 100 percent of the student health insurance premium for graduate assistants.

## Eligibility

Graduate assistants must be enrolled in at least 9 credit hours in fall and spring and at least 3 credit hours in summer, and must keep a cumulative grade point average of 3.0 or higher.

## Time off

Graduate assistants receive 10 days of paid time off per academic year. A graduate assistant who becomes a parent may take up to six weeks of paid parental accommodation.

## Evaluation and reappointment

Supervisors complete a written evaluation of each graduate assistant once a year, by May 15. Students are told whether they will be reappointed for the next academic year by April 15.

## Teaching assistants

New teaching assistants attend the TA training workshop before their first teaching term. International teaching assistants must hold an English proficiency certification before they lead a class or lab section. Unresolved workplace concerns can be taken to the Graduate Ombuds.
