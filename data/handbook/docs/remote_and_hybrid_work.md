# Remote and Hybrid Work Guidelines

*Brightwater Institute — Human Resources Guideline HR-310. Fictional sample document written for the RAG demo.*

## Eligibility

Staff in eligible positions may work remotely up to three days per week with the approval of their supervisor. A fully remote arrangement requires the approval of the divisional vice president and is reviewed every year. Remote work is a privilege tied to the position and to performance; it can be changed with 30 days of notice.

Every remote or hybrid arrangement is recorded in a Remote Work Agreement. Agreements are renewed each August.

## Working hours

Remote staff keep the same total hours as on-campus staff. All staff must be reachable during core hours, which run from 10:00 to 15:00 campus time, Monday through Friday. All staff are expected to work on campus during the first week of each semester.

## Equipment and costs

The Institute provides a laptop and one external monitor. Staff with an approved Remote Work Agreement receive a one-time home office stipend of 300 dollars for ergonomic equipment. Home internet service and utilities are not reimbursed.

## Working from other locations

Working from another state for more than 30 days in a calendar year requires advance approval from Human Resources and Payroll because of tax withholding rules. Working remotely from outside the country is limited to 14 days per calendar year and requires approval from the Office of Research Security.

## Data protection

Remote staff follow the Information Security policy at all times. Restricted (Level 4) data may be accessed from a home network only through the Vault virtual desktop. Printing Confidential or Restricted material at home is not allowed.
