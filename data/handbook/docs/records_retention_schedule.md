# Records Retention Schedule

*Brightwater Institute — Office of the General Counsel. Fictional sample document written for the RAG demo.*

## Retention periods

Financial records, including invoices and expense reports, are kept for seven years. Personnel files are kept for six years after the employee leaves the Institute. Student academic transcripts are kept permanently. Admission applications from applicants who did not enroll are kept for two years.

Grant and contract records are kept for three years after the final report is submitted, or for the period required by the sponsor, whichever is longer. Minutes of the governing board are kept permanently. Security camera footage is kept for 30 days unless it is needed for an investigation.

## Legal holds

When the Office of the General Counsel issues a legal hold, the destruction of all records covered by the hold stops until the hold is released in writing.

## Destruction

Records that have reached the end of their retention period are destroyed securely. Each destruction is entered in the destruction log and signed by the department's records coordinator.
