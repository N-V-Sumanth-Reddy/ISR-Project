# Emergency Closure and Severe Weather Policy

*Brightwater Institute — Office of Campus Operations Policy OPS-015. Fictional sample document written for the RAG demo.*

## Decision and announcement

The Vice President for Operations decides whether to close or delay the opening of campus. The decision is announced by 5:30 a.m. through BrightAlert text messages, the Institute website and the weather hotline at extension 5-0000. When a delayed opening is announced, campus opens at 10:00 a.m.

## Staff

Essential personnel report to work during a closure. They receive their regular pay plus compensatory time equal to one and a half times the hours they worked during the closure. Other staff receive their regular pay for the closure period and are not required to use leave.

## Classes and examinations

Instructors may not hold in-person classes during a closure. Examinations scheduled during a closure are rescheduled by the Registrar. Online classes may continue at the instructor's discretion, but attendance may not be required.

## Tornado warnings

During a tornado warning, everyone moves to the lowest interior floor of the building, away from windows. Outdoor warning sirens are tested at noon on the first Wednesday of each month.

## Research continuity

Each laboratory keeps a continuity plan that names the people responsible for animals, cell lines and equipment that cannot be left unattended.
