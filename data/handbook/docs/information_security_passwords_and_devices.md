# Information Security: Passwords and Devices

*Brightwater Institute — Information Security Office Standard IS-102. Fictional sample document written for the RAG demo.*

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made of several words are recommended. Multi-factor authentication is required for every Institute account. Passwords for ordinary accounts are changed only when a compromise is suspected; routine expiry is no longer used. Passwords for privileged administrator accounts are changed every 180 days.

## Institute-owned devices

Institute laptops use full-disk encryption and lock the screen after 10 minutes of inactivity. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT Service Desk at extension 5-4357 within four hours of discovering the loss, so that the device can be locked and wiped remotely.

Software is installed from the Software Center. Local administrator rights are granted by exception and expire after 12 months.

## Personal devices

Personal phones, tablets and computers may be used to read Institute email and to work with Public (Level 1) and Internal (Level 2) data, provided the device has a passcode and a supported, up-to-date operating system. Confidential and Restricted data may not be stored on personal devices.

## Patching and removable media

Critical security patches are installed within 7 days of release, and all other patches within 30 days. Unencrypted removable media, such as USB drives, may not hold Confidential or Restricted data.

## Phishing and training

Suspicious messages are reported with the Report Phish button in the mail client. All employees complete security awareness training every year by October 31. Accounts of employees who miss the deadline are restricted until the training is complete.

## Networks

Employees connect to the BW-Secure wireless network. Visitors use BW-Guest, which has no access to internal systems.
