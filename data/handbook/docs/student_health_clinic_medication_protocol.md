# Student Health Clinic: Medication Administration Protocol

*Brightwater Institute — Student Health Clinic nursing protocol NP-07. Fictional sample document written for the RAG demo. It is not medical guidance.*

## Scope

This protocol applies to nursing staff who give or supervise medication in the Student Health Clinic. It does not replace a prescriber's order.

## Missed or late doses in general

A dose given more than two hours after its scheduled time is recorded as a late dose. For time-critical medication the nurse notifies the prescriber before giving a late dose.

## Missed insulin dose

When a patient has missed a scheduled insulin dose, the nurse checks the patient's blood glucose immediately. The nurse does not give a double dose to make up for the missed one. Before any correction dose is given, the nurse contacts the on-call clinician at extension 5-2020 and follows the clinician's instructions. The event is documented in the chart, and a medication variance report is filed within 24 hours. A patient who is vomiting, confused, or whose meter reads HI is transferred to emergency care.

## Storage

Insulin is stored in the medication refrigerator at 2 to 8 degrees Celsius. Refrigerator temperatures are logged twice a day. An opened insulin vial is discarded 28 days after it was first opened, and the opening date is written on the label.

## Controlled substances and emergency supplies

Controlled substances are counted at every shift change by two nurses together. Epinephrine auto-injectors are kept at each nurse station and their expiry dates are checked monthly.

## After hours

When the clinic is closed, students reach the after-hours nurse line through the campus operator.
