# Laboratory Safety Manual (Excerpt)

*Brightwater Institute — Environmental Health and Safety (EHS). Fictional sample document written for the RAG demo.*

## Training

All laboratory personnel must complete General Laboratory Safety training before they begin work in a laboratory. Refresher training is required every two years. Personnel who work with particularly hazardous substances complete chemical-specific training every year.

## Personal protective equipment

The minimum protective equipment in any laboratory where chemicals are used is a lab coat, safety glasses and closed-toe shoes. Gloves are selected for the chemical in use. Lab coats are not worn outside the laboratory.

## Chemical storage and waste

Flammable liquids are stored in approved flammable storage cabinets. No more than 10 gallons of flammable liquids may be kept outside a cabinet in a single laboratory. Acids and bases are stored separately. Hazardous waste containers must be picked up within 90 days of the accumulation start date written on the label. Pickups are requested through EHS Assist.

## Emergency equipment

Laboratory staff flush eyewash stations weekly and record the check on the tag. Facilities staff test safety showers monthly. Fume hoods are certified every year, and the sash must not be raised above 18 inches while work is in progress.

## Incidents

Any injury must be reported to the supervisor immediately and to EHS within 24 hours using form EHS-12. For a serious injury or a fire, call the campus emergency line at extension 5-9111. Spills larger than one liter of a hazardous liquid are handled by the EHS spill team and not by laboratory staff.

## Working alone and visitors

Working alone is not permitted for procedures involving pyrophoric materials or other high-hazard operations. After-hours work uses a buddy system. Minors under 16 are not allowed in laboratories. Visitors aged 16 or 17 need written parental consent and direct supervision.
